## Excluded (not breakaways)

- **african-instituted-churches** (`african-instituted-churches`): Scholarly category, not a parent church; Kimbanguist/Aladura reattached to concrete mission contexts.
- **assyrian-church-of-the-east** (`assyrian-church-of-the-east`): Duplicate naming of the same historic Church of the East patriarchate; canonical catalog id is church-of-the-east (Assyrian Church of the East is the common modern English name for that body).
- **cathars** (`cathars`): Cathar/Albigensian networks are contested as a durable ‘church’; excluded under the formal breakaway definition (see root README / clean_breakaways).
- **congregationalist-approx** (`congregationalist-approx`): Synthetic milieu node, not a historical church people broke from.
- **eastern-catholic-note** (`eastern-catholic-note`): Eastern Catholic churches (Byzantine, Maronite, Chaldean, Syro-Malabar, etc.) are in full communion with Rome; not breakaways.
- **maronites-note** (`maronites-note`): Maronite Church is an Eastern Catholic church in full communion with Rome; not a breakaway.
- **messianic-jewish** (`messianic-jewish`): Modern Messianic congregations are not a schism from Baptist churches; mixed evangelical parentage — removed rather than assert a false parent.
- **oriental-orthodox** (`oriental-orthodox`): Modern family name for non-Chalcedonian miaphysite churches. Not a church polity that groups broke from; dogmatic rupture is Chalcedon (see each national church). Tracked under family_labels when present.
- **oriental-orthodox-twahedo-split-note** (`oriental-orthodox-twahedo-split-note`): Synthetic alias of eritrean-orthodox; removed as redundant.
- **pietist-movements** (`pietist-movements`): Pietism was largely an intra-Lutheran renewal; not a single durable separated communion.
- **protestant-missions-umbrella** (`protestant-missions-umbrella`): Synthetic umbrella, not a church; children reattached to real parents.
- **sspx** (`sspx`): Society of St. Pius X claims Catholic identity and the same communion; Rome treats it as irregular, not a separate church. Excluded under the formal definition (rejecting implementation while claiming same communion).
