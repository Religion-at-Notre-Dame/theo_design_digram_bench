# Council documents

Citation-preserving ecumenical council texts for Step 1, extracted from
[GibbilyGooo/catholic-doctrine-magisterium-rag-corpus](https://github.com/GibbilyGooo/catholic-doctrine-magisterium-rag-corpus)
v1.0.0, then completed from Tanner / Hanover mirrors.

Each `.jsonl` line is one citable unit with fields such as `session`,
`canon`, `paragraph`, `chapter`, `canonical_citation`, `text`,
`text_status`, and `source_url`. Prefer `canonical_citation` when writing
question sentences.

**Text preference:** full decree text > edition summary > empty. Summaries
are kept when no free English full text is available.

### Incomplete (edition limits)

| Scope | What we have | What we do not have |
|---|---|---|
| **Constance** | Full Tanner decrees for major sessions (authority/Haec Sancta, Wyclif/Hus, Frequens, reforms, etc.) | ~28 sessions are **Tanner summaries only** (judicial process / internal administration). Tanner/COD states those acts are indicated but not printed. Checked papalencyclicals, Catholic Culture, and Documenta Catholica Omnia — same summaries. Fuller Latin acta: Hardt / Finke. |
| **Florence (Basel phase)** | Full text for substantive decrees and all Ferrara/Florence union material | A few Basel sessions are procedural **summaries** only (petition readings, receptions, deferrals). |
| **Lateran V** | Full constitutions in later sessions | Session 1 notes that convoking bulls were read (Mansi refs) but does not reproduce the bull bodies. |
| **Early councils (Nicaea I–Nicaea II)** | Creeds, definitions, canons, selected letters (NPNF / Tanner) | Full day-by-day session **acta** (primarily Greek/Latin in Mansi et al.). |
| **Constantinople III** | Tanner Exposition of faith (paragraph-split) | Separate disciplinary canons (none issued); full session acts. |

### Full text vs summary

Each English record has `text_status`:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `full` | Conciliar decree / canon / definition / chapter body |
| `summary` | Tanner (or similar) bracket editorial note describing a session or act without the full wording |
| `empty` | Should not occur; regenerators keep summaries rather than drop loci |

**Constance summary-only sessions (keep as summaries):** 2, 6–7, 9–11, 16–20, 22–27, 29–36, 38, 41–42.

**Haec Sancta:** full restored decree text is present under Session 5; a papalencyclicals e-text editorial aside (“famous Haec Sancta decree contradicting Vatican 1…”) is removed as non-conciliar.

### Provenance of supplements

- Eight councils absent from the upstream RAG release (Constantinople IV, Lateran I–III, Lyons I, Vienne, Constance, Lateran V): `scripts/scrape_missing_councils.py` (Tanner via Papal Encyclicals Online).

- `text_status` annotation + this report: `scripts/annotate_council_text_status.py`.
